Applicant privacy

Recruitment information, handled for application review.

This page explains the intended handling of information submitted through the XBPO careers form when the Google integration is configured.

Information collectedHow it is usedStorage and accessDocumentsQuestions

Information collected

The careers form requests contact details, role interest, experience, optional education and portfolio information, a short introduction if provided, and a CV attachment.

How it is used

The information is intended for recruitment review, contacting the applicant about their application, and assessing possible fit for relevant roles.

Storage and access

After the configured Google Apps Script integration is enabled, application data can be written to a private Google Sheet, CVs can be stored in a private Drive folder, and the configured HR inbox can receive a notification.

Documents

Applicants should submit a CV only. The form specifically asks applicants not to upload identity documents or unrelated sensitive documents.

Questions

Recruitment or privacy questions should be directed through the website contact route until XBPO confirms its final published contact details.
